2027 School Terms South Africa: Dates for All Four Terms

Public schools in South Africa open for Term 1 on 11 January 2027 for educators and 13 January 2027 for learners, and close for the year on 8 December for learners (10 December for educators). All four terms are below, with the school holidays between them.

These are the dates for public schools. Independent (private) schools set their own dates, so check with your school. Dates last checked on 26 October 2026 against the Department of Basic Education's 2027 Calendar for Public Schools (Government Gazette No. 52178, 25 February 2025). Weeks and school days per term

Terms 1, 3 and 4 each have 10 weeks and Term 2 has 12, which makes 42 weeks. The gazette gives 48 school days in Term 1, 56 in Term 2, 46 in Term 3 and 47 in Term 4 for learners: 197 in total. Educators have 201 (50, 56, 46 and 49).

Public school or independent school?

The dates above come from the Department of Basic Education's calendar for public schools. Independent schools set their own dates, which often differ. Ask your school for its calendar. For every 2027 public holiday and long weekend, see the South Africa 2027 calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Are there separate inland and coastal school calendars in 2027?

No. The Department of Basic Education's 2027 calendar for public schools gives one set of term dates, and the Western Cape Education Department's notice describes them as applying in all provinces. Independent schools set their own dates.

When do schools open in 2027?

Public schools open for Term 1 on Monday 11 January 2027 for educators and Wednesday 13 January 2027 for learners.

When does Term 2 start in 2027?

Term 2 starts on Tuesday 6 April 2027 and ends on Friday 25 June 2027 (public schools).

Are there extra school holidays in 2027?

Monday 26 April 2027 is a special school holiday for public schools (it is not a public holiday), and Freedom Day follows on Tuesday 27 April. Public holidays that fall in term time also close schools. Check your own school for any school-specific days.

How many school days are there in 2027?

The calendar for public schools gives 197 school days for learners and 201 for educators across 42 weeks.

What if a public holiday is added for 2027?

The President can declare an extra public holiday by proclamation. None had been announced for 2027 when these dates were last checked on 26 October 2026.
