Protect the last member

The member paid last carries the most risk: everyone else has already been paid. Many clubs ask early receivers to sign an acknowledgement, keep a small reserve, or set a clear rule for members who stop paying after their turn.

Questions

What is a rotating stokvel?

Each month every member contributes the same amount and one member receives the whole pot. The cycle ends when everyone has received once.

How big is each pot?

Monthly contribution × number of members. With 10 members at R1,000 the pot is R10,000 every month.

Is the random draw fair?

It uses a standard shuffle in your browser. Do it together on one screen and minute the order so nobody can dispute it later.
