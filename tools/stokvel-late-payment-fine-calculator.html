Keep it about the rule, not the person

Fines work when they are written down before anyone is late. Read the rule from the constitution, calculate, and minute the amount. Avoid discussing reasons in the group chat. Handle hardship requests privately with the committee.

Common fine rules in SA clubs

  • A flat amount per late month (for example R50).
  • A percentage of the contribution per late month (for example 10%).
  • A once-off fine plus the arrears, with a cap so debts do not spiral.

Questions

Can a stokvel charge fines?

Clubs commonly agree fines in their constitution. Only apply rules members agreed to in writing.

Should fines be capped?

A cap stops a member's debt growing past what they can realistically repay, which keeps them in the club. It's your club's choice.

What happens to fines at year end?

Most clubs add fines to the pool and share them, or use them for the year-end function. Minute the rule.
