Free stokvel & burial society calculators

No signup, no app. Each tool runs in your browser and gives a line you can paste into the minutes.

  • Stokvel payout calculator
    Free stokvel payout calculator for South African savings clubs. Enter members, monthly contribution, months, interest, fines and bank fees to see the total pool and each member's year-end payout.
  • Stokvel rotation schedule generator
    Free rotating stokvel (umgalelo) schedule generator. Paste member names, pick a start month and contribution, and get a printable month-by-month payout order with pot size. Download as CSV.
  • Stokvel late payment and fine calculator
    Work out what a stokvel member owes after missed contributions: arrears plus flat or percentage fines, with an optional cap. Free, no signup, meeting-ready summary line.
  • Burial society contribution calculator
    Free burial society contribution calculator for South African societies. Enter the monthly group cost, member families, admin percentage and reserve target to get a fair monthly contribution per member.
