How the calculation works

Expected contributions are members × monthly amount × months. We subtract what is still owed, add bank interest and fines, and take off bank or admin fees. The net pool divided by members is the equal share.

Many clubs do not pay an equal share to a member who is behind. If that is your rule, pay that member their own contributions minus arrears and fines, and split the rest among members who are up to date. Write the rule down before November so payout day is not a debate.

Before payout day checklist

  • Match the bank statement to your contribution sheet, month by month.
  • List every member with arrears and the amount owed.
  • Agree how fines and interest are split, and minute it.
  • Two signatories check the final payout list.

Questions

How is a stokvel payout calculated?

Add all contributions actually received, plus interest and fines, minus bank and admin fees. Divide by the number of members for an equal share, unless your constitution sets a different rule for members in arrears.

Should interest be shared equally?

That is your club's decision. Many clubs split interest equally; others split it by how much each member contributed. Minute whichever rule you choose.

Does this store my numbers?

No. The calculator runs in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved.
