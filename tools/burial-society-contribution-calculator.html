Burial society contribution calculator

Set a monthly contribution that actually covers your society's costs. Enter what the society pays each month, the number of member families, and how much you want in reserve. You get the monthly and yearly amount per member.

Runs in your browser. Nothing is sent or stored.

Why a reserve matters

Claims do not arrive evenly. A small reserve each month means the society can pay out quickly without an emergency collection. Review the amount at your AGM.

What this tool does not do

It does not quote insurance or compare policies. Get cover details from your registered provider. It only splits the costs your society already knows.

Questions

How much should a burial society charge per member?

Enough to cover the monthly group cost, admin and a reserve, divided by paying members. This calculator does that sum; your members approve the final amount.

How often should we review contributions?

At least once a year at the AGM, and whenever the provider changes the premium or membership changes a lot.

Is this insurance advice?

No. It is arithmetic only. Speak to a registered provider about cover.
