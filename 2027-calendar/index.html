South African public holidays 2027

The Act lists 12 public holidays. When one falls on a Sunday, the next Monday is also a public holiday (section 2(1)). In 2027 that happens twice, so there are 14 holiday dates.

Long weekends and bridge days in 2027

For people who work Monday to Friday. Your leave policy and your employer decide what you can take.

  • Long weekends: 1–3 January · 20–22 March · 26–29 March (Easter) · 7–9 August · 24–26 September · 25–27 December.
  • Bridge days (1 leave day = 4 days off): Monday 26 April (24–27 April) and Friday 17 December (16–19 December).
  • Biggest March break: 3 leave days (23–25 March) give 10 days off in a row, Saturday 20 March to Monday 29 March.
  • Year-end: 4 leave days (28–31 December) give 9 days off, 25 December to 2 January 2028. 1 January 2028 is a Saturday.

FAQ

How many public holidays does South Africa have in 2027?

The Public Holidays Act lists 12 public holidays. In 2027 two of them fall on a Sunday (Human Rights Day on 21 March and Day of Goodwill on 26 December), so Monday 22 March and Monday 27 December are also public holidays. That gives 14 dates in total.

When do schools open in January 2027?

Learners at public schools start on Wednesday 13 January 2027. Educators start on Monday 11 January; 11 and 12 January are administration days.

Are the 2027 school terms final?

Yes. The Minister of Basic Education published the 2027 Calendar for Public Schools in Government Gazette No. 52178 (Notice 5902) on 25 February 2025. It applies to public schools in all nine provinces. There is no separate inland and coastal calendar.

Why do some sites show Term 3 ending on 1 October 2027?

The gazette says Term 3 runs from 20 July to 22 September and Term 4 from 5 October to 8 December (10 December for educators). The summary page on gov.za showed 20 July to 1 October and 11 October to 10 December when we checked on 25 September 2026. Those dates do not match the gazette's own week and school-day counts, so this page follows the gazette.

Is Monday 26 April 2027 a public holiday?

No. It is a special school holiday for public schools only, between the weekend and Freedom Day (Tuesday 27 April). Most workplaces are open that day.

Do private schools use these term dates?

Not always. Independent schools set their own calendars, so check your school's dates. The public holidays apply to everyone.
