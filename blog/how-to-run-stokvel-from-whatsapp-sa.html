How to run a stokvel from WhatsApp (without losing the books)

Yes — you can run a stokvel from WhatsApp in South Africa. No — the group chat should not be your only contribution ledger. This guide shows a practical split: WhatsApp for people, a sheet for money truth.

What WhatsApp is good for

  • Contribution reminders and meeting dates
  • Voice notes for members who prefer talk over typing
  • Quick “payment received — thank you” acknowledgements
  • Sharing status totals only (not full ID numbers)

What it is bad for: arrears history, year-end payout math, and POPIA-safe storage of IDs and phone lists.

The two-channel operating model

  1. WhatsApp — reminders, votes, meeting logistics.
  2. Contribution tracker (Excel or Google Sheets) — paid / unpaid / arrears by month.
  3. Fine & loan log — so informal advances do not vanish.
  4. Year-end payout sheet — clear ZAR totals before money moves.
  5. Constitution one-pager — adaptable wording your members discuss (not legal advice; not a NASASA filing service).

Update the tracker when payment hits the account — not from a vague “sent” tick in chat.

Weekly loop (treasurer, ~15 minutes)

  1. Post the reminder in WhatsApp as usual.
  2. Open the master sheet; mark bank-confirmed payments.
  3. Follow up arrears privately (DM or call), not by shaming in the group.
  4. Once a month, share status totals with the committee.
  5. Backup the file; keep IDs off the group thread.

Before payout season

Walk the year-end payout sheet with the constitution rules on paper. Freeze contribution totals from your Excel tracker. Minute the distribution. Then pay out via your normal bank or cash process.

FAQ

Can we still use WhatsApp after buying a pack?

Yes. Keep WhatsApp for communication. Keep the register and payout totals in a sheet you own.

Do we need a paid stokvel app?

Not if a one-time template and your own bank account are enough. SaaS apps help when you want shared logins and push notifications.

Google Sheets or Excel?

Both — import the CSVs into either. Sheets is easier on phones; Excel is fine for one master laptop.

Will Plain Desk hold our money?

No. Instant download templates only.
