Stokvel Fine & Loan Register Meeting (South Africa)

Fine and loan arguments break groups the same way exits do: one member says “I already paid the fine,” another says “that advance was forgiven,” and the WhatsApp thread has three different totals.

This guide is for SA savings clubs, grocery stokvels, and burial societies that need a shared fine & loan dispute meeting file — constitution fine/loan rule placeholders plus contribution / fine / loan / net numbers — before the room votes or pays anyone out.

Lead with the fine & loan dispute meeting job, not “spreadsheet vs free.” Free PDFs and free trackers stay useful. The gap is walking into the room with one shared structure so the room argues from the same fine/loan figures.

When you need this meeting

  1. Open fines pile up — members dispute amounts, dates, or whether a fine was waived.
  2. Member loans / advances unpaid — group lent from the pot; repayment timeline is unclear.
  3. Year-end or exit payout blocked — net share depends on fines and loans still open.
  4. Trust reset after a fight — room needs a dated snapshot before anyone pays or votes.

What belongs in the shared fine/loan meeting file

  1. Constitution fine / loan / interest placeholders your club fills — who may fine, how loans are approved, how waivers are minuted.
  2. Member register with status (active / exiting / exited) — display names for the room; keep IDs off the group chat (POPIA common sense).
  3. Contribution & payout calendar (Excel / Google Sheets friendly) — totals that feed the net worksheet.
  4. Fine & loan log — date, member, reason/type, amount, paid/outstanding, notes the secretary can read aloud.
  5. Year-end / net worksheet (or interim close) — contributions minus open fines/loans before payout or exit math.
  6. Short order of operations — freeze books → list open fines/loans together → minute decisions → pay or chase separately using your own bank process.

Public constitutions often list fine and loan rules. That is correct and free. This page is about running the fine/loan dispute meeting with rules + numbers in one pack — not debt collection and not credit counselling.

How this differs from a blank fine column in a tracker

A contribution tracker with one “fines” column shows a number. A fine & loan meeting also needs the rules placeholders, the dated register lines the room can argue from, and the net worksheet that shows how open items change payouts.

Same meeting-pack shape as year-end, exit, or treasurer handover. Different agenda: open fines and advances, not one officer’s handoff or one member’s exit alone.
