Burial Society Treasurer Pack (South Africa)

A burial society treasurer pack keeps good-standing, contributions, claim-week checklists, and minutes in one place — so “who paid?” is not an argument in WhatsApp when a family needs support. Built for SA society treasurers who want calm books, not insurance software.

What breaks without a treasurer pack

Burial societies often run on notebooks and voice notes. That holds until a claim week, a new secretary, or an AGM. Then you need: who is in good standing, what the contribution history shows, and a calm checklist before any benefit money leaves the pot. Scattered chats create conflict; a shared register creates clarity.

What a burial society treasurer pack should include

  • Contribution / good-standing register — members, monthly amounts, paid status, arrears (CSV → Google Sheets or Excel)
  • Claims / burial benefit checklist — steps to walk before payout, using your society’s existing rules
  • Constitution one-pager — plain-language placeholders for discussion (not legal advice)
  • Meeting minutes — short record of decisions and claim votes
  • Year-end treasurer close — AGM-ready totals and handoff notes

You own the files. Plain Desk ships templates — not a bank, not a funeral policy, not access to your society’s money.

Claim week: books first, then your rules

  1. Confirm the member against the register and good-standing status.
  2. Walk the claims checklist against your constitution / AGM rules.
  3. Minute the decision; keep IDs and phones off public group chats (POPIA).
  4. Update the register after money moves — not weeks later from memory.

The pack organises paperwork. It does not pay claims or replace a licensed insurer if your group uses one.
