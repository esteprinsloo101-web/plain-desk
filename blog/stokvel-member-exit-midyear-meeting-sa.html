Stokvel Member Exit Mid-Year Meeting (South Africa)

Mid-year exits blow up for one reason: the group argues from memory. One person remembers “you get your contributions back.” Another remembers “no exits until December.” The chat history is incomplete. The spreadsheet only shows who paid last month.

This guide is for SA savings clubs, grocery stokvels, and burial societies that need a shared exit meeting file — constitution exit placeholders plus contribution / fine / loan / payout numbers — before anyone is paid out or struck off.

Lead with the exit meeting job, not “spreadsheet vs free.” Free guides and free trackers stay useful. The gap is walking into the room with one shared structure so the argument is about the written rule, not a WhatsApp guess.

When you need this meeting

  1. Voluntary exit mid-cycle — member wants out before year-end share-out.
  2. Arrears / non-payment exit — group must decide fine, suspension, or removal against written rules.
  3. Relocation or hardship exit — still needs the same books + exit clause, not a special vibe.
  4. Dispute about “what we said at join” — argue from the constitution + sheets, not five chats.

What belongs in the shared exit file

  1. Constitution exit placeholders your club fills — voluntary exit, expulsion triggers, refund vs forfeit, waiting periods, notice, vote threshold.
  2. Member register with join date and status (active / exiting / exited) — display names for the room; keep IDs off the group chat (POPIA common sense).
  3. Contribution calendar (Excel / Google Sheets friendly) — total paid to date by the exiting member and by the group.
  4. Fine & loan log — unpaid fines and open advances that affect the net figure.
  5. Net exit / payout worksheet — contributions minus fines/loans (and any rule your club wrote for interest or admin) before money moves.
  6. Short chair/secretary order of operations — notice → freeze books → apply written rule → record decision → pay or refuse per rule.

Public how-to guides often say “put exit rules in the constitution.” That is correct and free advice. This page is about running the exit meeting with rules + numbers in one pack — not a substitute for your club’s own signed constitution.

How this differs from year-end payout articles

Year-end guides focus on festive share-out pressure and cash-handling safety. Mid-year exits are a different meeting: one member’s ledger against the exit clause, while the rest of the club continues.

Same pack shape. Different agenda. Do not invent Google rankings from tool results — treat visibility as unknown until a manual SERP check proves otherwise.

No. It is a meeting pack: exit-rule placeholders plus contribution calendar, fine/loan log, and net exit worksheet so the room shares one structure.
