Gumroad to Google Sheet Automation

A Gumroad → Sheet automation blueprint shows digital sellers how to log each sale into a Google Sheet — product, price, fees, buyer email (if present), and date — so your ledger is not a pile of Gumroad emails. Built for solo operators who want Make or n8n steps, not a custom engineering project.

Why sellers need a sales ledger outside Gumroad

Gumroad’s dashboard is fine for payouts. It is not a calm weekly P&L. When you run several SKUs (stokvel packs, bookkeeping CSVs, Notion templates), you need one sheet that answers: what sold, what fees ate, and what to improve next week. Export-once-a-month is how refunds and fee surprises hide.

What the automation blueprint includes

  • Trigger map — when a sale happens (webhook / polling options)
  • Make / n8n step outline — fields to map into columns (SKU, net, fees, currency)
  • Ledger CSV starter — columns ready for ZAR-friendly bookkeeping
  • Weekly check ritual — reconcile sheet totals vs Gumroad payouts
  • Kill / pause notes — when to stop an automation that is noisy or wrong

You own the Sheet and the automation account. Plain Desk ships the blueprint — not hosted bots or access to your store.

A simple setup path

  1. Duplicate the ledger CSV into Google Sheets; freeze the header row.
  2. Create a Make or n8n scenario from the step outline; map sale fields to columns.
  3. Run one test sale (or Gumroad ping) and confirm a row lands correctly.
  4. Every Friday, spot-check totals against Gumroad before your weekly review.

Start with logging only. Fancy dashboards can wait until rows are trustworthy.
