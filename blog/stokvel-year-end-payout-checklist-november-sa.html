Stokvel Year-End Payout Checklist (November, South Africa)

December payout fights usually start in November — when the books are still a WhatsApp scroll. This checklist is for SA savings clubs, grocery stokvels, and burial societies that keep records (not insurance, not a bank).

You do not need a ranking. You need a minute-able sheet before money moves.

Why November, not December

If you wait until DecWhat breaks
Contributions still “open”Last-minute cash claims with no paper trail
Fines/loans live in chatOne person’s memory becomes the dispute
No bank vs sheet checkPool total and bank balance disagree at the table
No constitution walk-throughRules argued after cash is counted

Open the year-end sheet at least one committee meeting before payout day.

10-step checklist (print or share PDF)

  1. Freeze the member list — who is in for this cycle’s share-out (per constitution).
  2. Freeze historical contributions — stop editing old paid flags; dated notes only for corrections.
  3. Close the fine & loan log — informal advances that only one person remembered become fights.
  4. Match bank / cash pot to the sheet — difference = investigate before payout, not after.
  5. Agree the distribution rule — equal share, rotation already paid, grocery vouchers, etc. (as constitution says).
  6. Compute net ZAR per member — contributions − fines + loan repayments − advances (your rules).
  7. Export a PDF for minutes — totals/status only; keep full IDs off the group chat (POPIA common sense).
  8. Minute the meeting — who approved the sheet, payout date, dual-signatory plan.
  9. Pay out through the club’s bank/cash process — the sheet does not transfer money.
  10. Start next year’s tracker clean — new cycle file; archive the closed year.

Spreadsheet vs app (honest line)

Apps and free trackers help with “who paid this month?”. Year-end share-out is a different job: freeze books, clear arrears, minute net amounts. Many clubs use sheets (or a one-time treasurer pack) for the audit trail even if they also use an app for reminders.

Burial societies

Keep claim / good-standing urgency separate from grocery or savings share-outs. This checklist is for books — not funeral cover and not claim payouts.

FAQ

When should we start?

Late October / November for calendar-year clubs — before festive spend pressure.

Excel or Google Sheets?

Either. Export PDF for minutes.

Does the checklist pay members?

No. Your bank (or cash process) does. The checklist + sheet are the agreed record.
