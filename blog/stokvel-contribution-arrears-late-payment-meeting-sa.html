Stokvel Contribution Arrears Meeting (South Africa)

Late contributions break groups before fines and loans do: one member says “I paid on the 7th,” another says “the rule is the 5th,” and the WhatsApp thread has three different arrears totals.

Lead with the contribution arrears meeting job, not “spreadsheet vs free.” Free PDFs and free trackers stay useful. The gap is walking into the room with one shared structure so the room argues from the same late-payment figures.

This is not the fine & loan dispute meeting. That agenda is open fines and advances already on the books. This agenda is who missed or paid late this cycle (and what the agreed late rule says) before those lines become a longer fight.

When you need this meeting

  1. Several members paid after the deadline — room disputes who is “late” vs “on time.”
  2. Arrears block a payout turn — constitution says late payers skip or wait; figures disagree.
  3. Repeated defaults — group must decide fine, suspension, or exit path from shared numbers.
  4. Trust reset mid-year — freeze the books on one date so WhatsApp screenshots stop arguing.

What belongs in the shared arrears meeting file

  1. Constitution late-payment / default placeholders your club fills — deadline day, late fee (if any), how many misses before suspension, how waivers are minuted.
  2. Member register with status (active / exiting / exited) — display names for the room; keep IDs off the group chat (POPIA common sense).
  3. Contribution & payout calendar (Excel / Google Sheets friendly) — cycle columns up to the meeting cutoff.
  4. Arrears / late list — member, cycle, due date, amount due, amount paid, days late (or “not paid”), notes the secretary can read aloud.
  5. Fine & loan log (if the room already applied late fees) — so new late fees do not double-count old ones.
  6. Net / interim worksheet — contributions minus open late fees or other deductions before anyone’s turn or exit math.
  7. Short order of operations — freeze books at cutoff → list late/unpaid together → apply only rules already in the constitution → minute decisions → bank separately.

Public constitutions often list late-payment and attendance rules. That is correct and free. This page is about running the arrears meeting with rules + numbers in one pack — not debt collection and not credit counselling.

How this differs from a tracker “arrears” count

A contribution tracker that flags arrears shows a count. An arrears meeting also needs the late-payment rule placeholders, the dated line-by-line list the room can argue from, and the net worksheet that shows how late fees change payouts.

Same meeting-pack shape as year-end, exit, treasurer handover, or fine/loan register. Different agenda: who paid late or not at all this cycle, not open loans alone or one officer’s handoff.

FAQ

Is this just another blank spreadsheet?

No. It is a meeting pack: late-payment rule placeholders plus contribution calendar, arrears list, and net worksheet so the room shares one cutoff-date structure.

Our constitution already mentions late fees — why buy a pack?

Rule wording without a dated arrears list still ends in “prove it from the chat.” The pack puts placeholders and the sheets in one download. Official free constitution PDFs stay free elsewhere. This is not a filing service and not a debt collector.

Is this the same as the fine & loan meeting guide?

No. Fine/loan meetings argue open fines and advances already logged. Arrears meetings argue this cycle’s late or missing contributions first. Many clubs run both; use the agenda that matches the fight.

Excel or Google Sheets?

Spreadsheet templates work in either. Pick one master file the secretary owns for the meeting date.

Do you hold our money?

No. Templates only. You keep banking and member data.
