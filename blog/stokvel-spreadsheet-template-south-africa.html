Stokvel Spreadsheet Template South Africa

A stokvel spreadsheet template keeps contributions, members, and payouts in one place instead of scattered WhatsApp messages. South African treasurers usually need Excel or Google Sheets files they can own offline — not another login that holds the club’s money.

What a useful SA stokvel spreadsheet must include

Skip decorative dashboards. Cover these four ledgers:

1. Member register

Columns that actually get used:

  • Member ID / number
  • Full name
  • Join date
  • Phone (private — POPIA)
  • Status (active / exited)
  • Notes (beneficiary contact if your club uses one)

2. Contribution tracker

  • Contribution amount and cycle (weekly / monthly)
  • Due date
  • Paid Y/N
  • Amount received
  • Method (cash / EFT) — recorded by you; the sheet is not a payment app
  • Arrears flag

3. Fine & loan log

Many clubs mix “late fine” and small internal loans in chat. A separate log prevents silent debt:

  • Date · member · type (fine / loan) · amount · due · settled Y/N

4. Payout calendar / year-end sheet

Especially for grocery and savings clubs aiming at December / January:

  • Rotation order or share calculation
  • Expected payout date
  • Total pool vs deductions
  • Sign-off initials at AGM

Excel vs Google Sheets (practical)

OptionBest whenWatch-outs
Google SheetsShared view with secretary; phone accessDon’t paste the full sheet into a public WhatsApp group
Excel / LibreOfficeOne laptop “master”; offline meetingsBackup weekly to Drive or email

CSVs import into both. One treasurer owns the master; others get PDF exports for meetings.

Common mistakes that break trust

  1. Only recording totals — you cannot answer “Did Thabo pay March?”
  2. Editing in the WhatsApp group — no audit trail; privacy risk
  3. No exit column — exited members stay in the payout list by accident
  4. Mixing burial claims language into a savings sheet — burial societies need contribution records; claims and insurance are separate
  5. No backup — phone loss = lost season

Minimal setup in one evening

  1. Create four tabs (or four CSVs): Members, Contributions, FinesLoans, YearEnd.
  2. Freeze header rows; protect the “amount due” cell so it doesn’t drift.
  3. After each meeting, mark Paid Y/N the same day.
  4. Before payout season, copy contribution totals into the year-end sheet and reconcile with the bank statement you control.
  5. Store the master privately; share summaries only.

FAQ

What is the best stokvel spreadsheet template for South Africa?

The best one is the file your treasurer will update every cycle: member register + contribution tracker + payout sheet. Fancy charts are optional.

Can I use Google Sheets on my phone?

Yes. Keep the master link private. Export PDF for group announcements instead of sharing edit access widely.

Does a spreadsheet replace a constitution?

No. Rules belong in a constitution document; money movements belong in the spreadsheet. Many groups need both.

Is this the same as a stokvel banking app?

No. Spreadsheet templates organise records. Your group still uses its own bank account or cash process. Templates do not hold funds.
