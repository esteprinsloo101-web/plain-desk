Stokvel Year-End / Dispute Meeting Pack (South Africa)

Year-end and dispute meetings fail the same way: rules live in one WhatsApp thread, numbers live in another, and nobody can prove what the group already agreed.

This guide is for SA savings clubs, grocery stokvels, and burial societies that need a shared meeting file — constitution placeholders plus contribution / fine / loan / payout sheets — before money moves or a fight hardens.

Lead with the meeting job, not “spreadsheet vs free.” Free PDFs and free trackers stay useful for what they are. The gap is walking into the room with one shared structure.

When you need this meeting

  1. November / early December share-out — freeze books before festive payout pressure.
  2. Member exit mid-year — exit payout rules must match the sheet, not a memory.
  3. Loan / advance from the pot — fine & loan log + constitution wording in the same pack.
  4. Dispute about arrears or “who said what” — argue from one file, not five chats.

What belongs in the shared file

  1. Constitution placeholders your club fills — membership, contributions, payout/rotation, exit, loans/emergencies, meetings, disputes.
  2. Member register (display names for the room; keep IDs off the group chat — POPIA common sense).
  3. Contribution & payout calendar (Excel / Google Sheets friendly).
  4. Fine & loan log for advances and penalties your rules allow.
  5. Year-end payout sheet — net contributions, fines, and loans before payout day.
  6. Short treasurer how-to so the chair and secretary use the same order of operations.

How this differs from bank “stay safe at payout” articles

Public guides about year-end stokvels often focus on withdrawal safety (EFT vs cash, staggering withdrawals, no social posts). Those tips matter. They are a different job from running the meeting with frozen books and written rules.

This page is about the meeting file — not cash-handling advice and not a bank product. Observations from public search tools are not invented rankings.

FAQ

Is this just another blank spreadsheet?

No. It is a meeting pack: constitution placeholders plus contribution calendar, fine/loan log, and year-end payout sheet so the room shares one structure.

We already have a free constitution PDF — why buy a pack?

Free PDFs do not give you the operational sheets for contributions, fines/loans, and year-end net payout in one place. Official templates stay free elsewhere if you need them. This pack is the treasurer toolkit for the meeting, not a filing service.

Excel or Google Sheets?

Spreadsheet templates work in either. Pick one master file the secretary owns.

Will this stop payout fights?

No guarantees. It gives one shared pack of rules + numbers. Not legal or financial advice.
