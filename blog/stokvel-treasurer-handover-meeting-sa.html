Stokvel Treasurer Handover Meeting (South Africa)

Treasurer changes break groups the same way exits do: the old books live on one phone, the bank signatories live in another head, and the WhatsApp group only remembers “we voted yesterday.”

This guide is for SA savings clubs, grocery stokvels, and burial societies that need a shared treasurer handover meeting file — constitution officer/signatory placeholders plus contribution / fine / loan / payout numbers — before the new treasurer touches the pot.

Lead with the handover meeting job, not “spreadsheet vs free.” Free PDFs and free trackers stay useful. The gap is walking into the room with one shared structure so outgoing and incoming treasurers argue from the same file.

When you need this meeting

  1. Voluntary resign / term end — treasurer steps down at AGM or mid-year.
  2. Vote to replace officers — group elects a new treasurer; books must follow.
  3. Illness / relocation — temporary or permanent handoff with frozen figures.
  4. Trust reset after a dispute — room needs a dated snapshot before anyone else signs.

What belongs in the shared handover file

  1. Constitution officer / signatory placeholders your club fills — who may collect, who must co-sign, how leadership changes are minuted.
  2. Member register with status (active / exiting / exited) — display names for the room; keep IDs off the group chat (POPIA common sense).
  3. Contribution & payout calendar (Excel / Google Sheets friendly) — totals to the handover date.
  4. Fine & loan log — open advances and unpaid fines that affect net figures.
  5. Year-end / net worksheet (or interim close) — contributions minus fines/loans before the new treasurer starts.
  6. Short order of operations — freeze books → count/reconcile together → minute the handoff → update bank mandate separately with your bank’s own forms.

Public constitutions often list treasurer duties (records, deposit slips, co-signatures). That is correct and free. This page is about running the handover meeting with rules + numbers in one pack — not a substitute for your bank’s signatory change process.

How this differs from AGM minutes-only packs

Agenda / attendance / resolutions packs record what the room decided. A treasurer handover also needs the books that prove contributions, fines, loans, and net position on the handoff date.

Same meeting-pack shape as year-end or exit. Different agenda: one officer’s books, not one member’s exit payout.

FAQ

Is this just another blank spreadsheet?

No. It is a meeting pack: officer/signatory placeholders plus contribution calendar, fine/loan log, and net worksheet so the room shares one handover structure.

Our constitution already names a treasurer — why buy a pack?

A role name without books still ends in “prove the balance from the chat.” The pack puts placeholders and the sheets in one download. Official free constitution PDFs stay free elsewhere. This is not a filing service and not a bank mandate form.

Excel or Google Sheets?

Spreadsheet templates work in either. Pick one master file the secretary owns for the handoff date.

Will this stop handoff fights?

No guarantees. It gives one shared pack of rules + numbers. Not legal or financial advice.
