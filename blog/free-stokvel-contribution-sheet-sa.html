How to use the free sheet in one meeting

  1. Open the tracker and fill club header fields.
  2. List every member currently in good standing.
  3. Backfill months already paid this year (or start clean from this month).
  4. Agree who edits the master (one treasurer) and where the PDF backup lives.
  5. If rules / fines / payout maths still live in chat — look at Stokvel OS next.
