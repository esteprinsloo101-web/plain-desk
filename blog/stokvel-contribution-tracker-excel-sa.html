Stokvel contribution tracker in Excel (South Africa)

Many SA treasurers still prefer Excel over a WhatsApp thread or a monthly SaaS fee. A stokvel contribution tracker Excel file answers one question every cycle: who paid, who is in arrears, and what the pool looks like in ZAR before payout season.

Why Excel still wins for small clubs

  • One-time file you own — no login fees
  • Works offline on a shared laptop
  • Easy PDF export for meeting minutes
  • Import the same CSVs into Google Sheets later if phones need access

Excel is the workbook. WhatsApp stays the reminder channel — not the ledger.

Minimum columns for an SA contribution sheet

Club header (set once)

  • Club / stokvel name
  • Contribution amount (ZAR)
  • Cycle (weekly / monthly)
  • Treasurer + cycle start date

Per member, per cycle

  • Member name (keep IDs private — POPIA-aware)
  • Expected amount · amount received · Paid (Y/N)
  • Date received · bank / cash reference
  • Arrears carried forward

Totals row — expected pool, collected, shortfall. Those three numbers feed your year-end payout sheet.

15-minute Excel rhythm

  1. Open the master workbook (not a chat export).
  2. Mark every payment that hit the club account since last session.
  3. List arrears for private follow-up — do not dump full IDs in the group.
  4. Snapshot totals into minutes or a PDF.
  5. Save a dated backup.

Miss a week and trust erodes — same as a cash book left open.

From tracker to payout

Before December / January: freeze contribution totals, apply fines and loans from your log, move net figures into a year-end payout sheet, minute the distribution, then pay out via your normal bank process. The sheet records; it does not transfer money.
