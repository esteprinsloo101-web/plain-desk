SA Phone-Business Admin Kit

A SA phone-business admin kit is a small set of sheets and scripts for operators who sell from WhatsApp and phone calls — not from a fancy CRM. Quotes, invoices, a simple client list, and payment follow-ups you can run from a phone or laptop.

Why phone-first SMEs lose money in the chat thread

When quotes live in voice notes and invoices are “I’ll send later,” cash leaks. South African side hustles and micro businesses often close deals on WhatsApp — then forget who owes what. Admin is not bureaucracy; it is how you get paid without chasing every Sunday night.

What belongs in a phone-business admin kit

  • Quote sheet — line items, VAT-friendly totals, validity date
  • Invoice / receipt log — number, client, amount, paid / unpaid
  • Client mini-register — name, WhatsApp, last job, notes
  • WhatsApp setup checklist — business hours, pinned price list, payment details
  • Payment follow-up scripts — polite, short, copy-paste (ZAR / EFT / PayFast / card links)

CSV or Google Sheets is enough. You do not need another SaaS login to send a R450 quote.

A calm weekly rhythm (20 minutes)

  1. Export or note this week’s quotes sent and invoices unpaid (5 min).
  2. Send one follow-up to each overdue client (5–10 min).
  3. Update the client register with next action dates (5 min).
  4. Pin or refresh your WhatsApp price / payment message if anything changed.

Do this every week and “I’ll invoice later” stops eating your weekend.
