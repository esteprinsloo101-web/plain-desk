Stokvel Contribution Tracker (Excel/Sheets)

A stokvel contribution tracker is a simple ledger that answers one question every cycle: who has paid, and who has not. Treasurers in South Africa use Excel or Google Sheets for this more often than full apps — especially when the club wants a one-time template, not a subscription.

Fields every tracker needs

Header (set once)

  • Club name
  • Contribution amount (ZAR)
  • Cycle (weekly / monthly)
  • Treasurer name
  • Cycle start date

Per member, per cycle

  • Member name / ID
  • Expected amount
  • Amount received
  • Paid (Y/N)
  • Date received
  • Reference (EFT ref or receipt no.)
  • Arrears carried

Totals row

  • Expected pool
  • Collected
  • Shortfall

Those three totals feed your year-end payout conversation.

Weekly rhythm (15 minutes)

  1. Open the master sheet (not a WhatsApp export).
  2. Mark every payment received since last session.
  3. List arrears privately for follow-up.
  4. Snapshot totals into meeting minutes or a PDF.
  5. Backup the file.

Miss a week and the tracker stops being trusted — same as a cash book.

Connecting tracker → payout calendar

Contribution data is only half the job. Before December / January:

  1. Freeze contribution totals for the year.
  2. Apply fines / loans from your fine-loan log.
  3. Move net figures into a year-end payout sheet.
  4. Agree distribution in a minuted meeting.
  5. Pay out via your normal bank or cash process — the sheet records; it does not transfer money.

Mistakes that cause fights

  • Changing historical paid flags without a note
  • Letting every member edit the sheet
  • Tracking only “who is next to receive” without tracking who paid in
  • Using screenshots as the only archive

Fix: one editor, dated notes column, monthly PDF export.

FAQ

What is a stokvel contribution tracker?

A spreadsheet (or similar ledger) that records expected vs received contributions per member each cycle.

Excel or Google Sheets — which is better?

Both work. Sheets is easier for phone viewing; Excel is fine for a single master laptop. Import CSV either way.

Can the tracker replace WhatsApp entirely?

Use WhatsApp for reminders if you must; keep records in the tracker. Chat is a poor audit trail.

Does Plain Desk process our contributions?

No. You collect and bank funds yourselves. The pack only organises paperwork.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No. Templates only. Your constitution and bank mandates remain your group’s responsibility.
