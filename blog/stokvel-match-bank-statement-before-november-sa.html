Match the bank statement before November

Payout week fights often start as a rand mismatch nobody caught in September. Before grocery or year-end payout week, take one meeting to compare the club’s records with the club bank statement.

Three checks for the treasurer

  1. Get the full statement. Print or download the club bank statement for last month — not a screenshot of “the balance.”
  2. Match every movement. Tick every deposit and fee against the contribution grid (and fine/loan log if you have one).
  3. Say the close out loud. At the next meeting, read the closing balance out loud and note who checked the statement.

Why this matters before November

A contribution grid can show who paid, but it does not prove what reached the club account. Bank fees, missed deposits, and old fine or loan entries can leave the books and the balance disagreeing. Finding the difference in a routine meeting gives the group a chance to check the source records before payout decisions are made.

If the room still argues from WhatsApp memory, write down the balance, the date of the statement, the person who checked it, and the next question to resolve. Keep the master records with the treasurer and share only what the group needs.
