Stop running your stokvel from WhatsApp chaos

Most South African stokvels and savings clubs start in a WhatsApp group. That works for chat — until December payout, a missed contribution, or a new treasurer inherits a thread of voice notes. WhatsApp chaos is when “who paid?” lives in memory, not in a register you can open in Sheets or Excel.

What WhatsApp chaos looks like in a real club

  • Contribution reminders buried under birthday posts and memes
  • “I paid — check the screenshot” with no date or member ID on a ledger
  • Fines and loans remembered by one person only
  • Year-end payout arguments because totals do not match the chat
  • POPIA risk when IDs and phone numbers get re-shared in the group

A group chat is a meeting room. It is not a contribution tracker.

What a calm stokvel OS replaces

A treasurer pack does not replace your bank account or your WhatsApp group. It replaces the books that should never have lived only in chat:

  • Constitution / rules one-pager — adaptable wording your members can discuss (not legal advice; not a NASASA filing service)
  • Member register — who is in, contact fields you keep private
  • Contribution tracker — paid / unpaid / arrears by month
  • Fine & loan log — so informal advances do not vanish
  • Year-end payout sheet — clear totals before money moves

Import CSVs into Google Sheets or Excel. You keep the money and the member data.

A simple weekly loop (still use WhatsApp)

  1. Post the reminder in WhatsApp as usual.
  2. Update the contribution tracker when payment hits the account — not from a vague “sent” tick.
  3. Once a month, screenshot or share status totals only (not full IDs) with the committee.
  4. Before payout season, walk the year-end sheet with the constitution rules on paper.

WhatsApp stays for communication. The sheet stays for truth.
