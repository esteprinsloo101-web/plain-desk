Stokvel year-end payout sheet (South Africa)

December and January are when SA stokvels fight — not because people are greedy, but because totals lived in WhatsApp. A year-end payout sheet freezes the books before money moves: contributions, fines, loans, and net ZAR per member in one place the committee can minute.

What the payout sheet must show

  • Member list matching the contribution tracker
  • Total contributed (year / cycle)
  • Fines owed and fines paid
  • Loans outstanding and repayments
  • Net amount due to (or from) each member
  • Club pool total vs bank balance check
  • Distribution rule (equal share, grocery vouchers, rotating payout — as your constitution says)

The sheet does not transfer money. Your bank (or cash process) does. The sheet is the agreed record.

Four steps before payout day

  1. Freeze contributions — stop editing historical paid flags; add a dated note if a correction is needed.
  2. Close the fine & loan log — informal advances that only one person remembered become fights.
  3. Walk the sheet with the constitution — rules on paper beat memory in the group chat.
  4. Minute the meeting — export PDF totals (status only; keep full IDs off the group).

Common payout mistakes

  • Paying out from chat screenshots instead of a ledger
  • Letting every member edit the master sheet the week before payout
  • Ignoring arrears until cash is already on the table
  • Mixing burial-claim urgency with savings-club grocery payout without separate books

FAQ

When should we open the year-end payout sheet?

At least one committee meeting before money moves — usually late November / early December for calendar-year clubs.

Excel or Google Sheets?

Both. Import Stokvel OS CSVs into either; export a PDF for minutes.

Does the sheet pay members automatically?

No. You keep banking. The sheet records agreed net amounts.

What if we only have WhatsApp history?

Rebuild a tracker from bank statements where possible, then start the next cycle on a proper sheet. See how to run a stokvel from WhatsApp.
