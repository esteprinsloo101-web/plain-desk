A digital seller ledger keeps Gumroad sales, platform fees, expenses, and payouts in one place — so you know what actually landed in ZAR after fees, not just what the dashboard showed last month. Built for SA solo sellers who want calm Gumroad bookkeeping, not accounting software.

Why digital sellers need a ledger outside Gumroad

Gumroad shows sales and payouts. It does not answer: what did each SKU net after fees, what ads or tools ate the margin, and what to cut next week. When you sell several templates (stokvel packs, Notion OS, automation blueprints), one sheet that lists sales, fees, expenses, and payouts stops “I think it was fine” from becoming a surprise shortfall.

What a digital seller ledger should track

  • Sales rows — date, SKU / product name, gross amount, currency
  • Fees — Gumroad / payment fees so net per sale is visible
  • Expenses — ads, tools, design, or other costs tied to the month
  • Payouts — when money hit your bank, and the amount in ZAR
  • Weekly / monthly totals — so you can spot fee creep and weak SKUs

You own the CSVs and Sheets. Plain Desk ships the ledger templates — not hosted bookkeeping, not access to your store.

A simple weekly bookkeeping loop

  1. Export or paste new Gumroad sales into the sales sheet; fill fee columns.
  2. Log any tools, ads, or one-off costs in the expenses sheet for that week.
  3. When a payout lands, record date and ZAR amount against the period.
  4. Friday: glance at net by SKU and expenses — keep, kill, or improve one thing.

Start with honest rows. Fancy charts can wait until the numbers are trustworthy.
