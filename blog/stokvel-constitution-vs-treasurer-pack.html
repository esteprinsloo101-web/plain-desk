Stokvel Constitution vs Treasurer Pack

A free stokvel constitution template gives your group written rules. A treasurer pack pairs adaptable constitution wording with spreadsheets so contributions, members, and year-end payouts stay visible outside WhatsApp.

Why free constitution PDFs rank — and what they miss

Search for “stokvel constitution template” and you will usually find official or bank-style PDFs (including NASASA resources). Those documents are useful when your group needs a starting skeleton for rules, meetings, and member duties.

They do not usually give you:

  • A live member register you can filter
  • A contribution tracker (who paid, who is late)
  • A payout calendar or year-end distribution sheet
  • A fine / loan log tied to the same members

Treasurers still end up in notebooks and chat threads after the PDF is signed.

What belongs in a treasurer pack (operations)

Once rules exist, books must match them. A practical SA treasurer pack typically includes:

  1. Constitution template — editable wording you adapt in a meeting
  2. Member register — names, join date, contact, status (CSV → Excel / Google Sheets)
  3. Contribution & payout calendar — cycle dates, paid Y/N, whose turn
  4. Fine & loan log — arrears and small loans in one place
  5. Year-end payout sheet — clearer December / January distributions
  6. Short how-to — so the next treasurer can take over

That combination matches how most grocery stokvels, savings clubs, and burial societies actually run: rules on paper, money movements in a ledger.

How to decide in one meeting

  1. Ask: “Where do we record who paid last month?” If the answer is chat, you need a tracker.
  2. Ask: “Can we show a clean member list and payout totals at AGM?” If not, add register + year-end sheet.
  3. Keep banking with your chosen bank or society account — templates never hold funds.
  4. Keep member data private (POPIA): master sheets stay with the treasurer; share PDF summaries, not full contact dumps in public groups.

FAQ

Is a free NASASA constitution enough on its own?

It can be enough for rules. Most groups still need separate spreadsheets for contributions and payouts. A pack combines adaptable wording with those sheets; it does not replace official NASASA resources when your group requires them.

Does Plain Desk Stokvel OS replace NASASA registration?

No. It is a digital download for treasurers — templates and records — not a filing service, not legal advice, and not a substitute for any official body.

Is this a bank or insurance product?

No. It does not hold money, process payments, or pay funeral claims. Your group keeps its own banking and any insurance arrangements.
