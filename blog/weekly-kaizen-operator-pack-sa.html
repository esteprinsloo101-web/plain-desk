Weekly Kaizen Template South Africa

A weekly kaizen template for South African digital sellers is a short Friday review — not a productivity cult. You list what shipped, what blocked revenue, what to kill, and one experiment for next week. The goal is a calm operator rhythm you can repeat in under 30 minutes.

Why digital sellers need a weekly review

Gumroad, Notion, and Sheets hustles stall when “busy” replaces “measured.” A digital seller weekly review answers four questions:

  1. What moved money or audience this week?
  2. What bottleneck repeated (ads, listing copy, fulfilment, support)?
  3. What should we stop doing?
  4. What is the single focus for next week?

Without a written review, WhatsApp notes and browser tabs become the archive — and nothing compounds.

What belongs in a weekly kaizen pack

  • Friday review sheet — wins, misses, metrics snapshot (ZAR sales, refunds, hours)
  • Bottleneck log — recurring friction with owner + next action
  • Kill criteria — when a SKU, channel, or experiment is dead
  • Backlog — parking lot so shiny ideas do not hijack the week
  • Operator scorecard — simple 1–5 scores (shipping, focus, money hygiene)

CSV or spreadsheet worksheets are enough. You do not need another SaaS login.

A 25-minute Friday rhythm

  1. Export or note this week’s sales and fees (5 min).
  2. Fill the review: shipped / blocked / learned (10 min).
  3. Update bottlenecks and apply kill criteria (5 min).
  4. Pick one focus + one experiment; park the rest (5 min).

Do this every Friday and your Monday starts with a written plan — not inbox panic.
