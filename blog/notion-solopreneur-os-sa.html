A Notion solopreneur template SA is a one-role cockpit: one dashboard for this week’s focus, sales pipeline, content queue, kill rules, and money hygiene. Built for South African digital sellers who sell from a laptop — not for agencies with five workspaces.

Why solopreneurs drown in empty Notion pages

Blank databases feel productive until Monday. Solo operators need fewer pages, clearer defaults, and a weekly loop that ties content → sales → cash. If your Notion is a museum of unused templates, start over with one role: operator.

What a solid Notion Solopreneur OS includes

  • Weekly dashboard — focus, metrics snapshot, top three tasks
  • Sales board — offers, leads, follow-ups (Gumroad / DMs / email)
  • Content queue — ideas → draft → published, with a kill column
  • Kill rules — written criteria so zombie projects die on schedule
  • Money hygiene — simple ZAR-friendly checklist (fees, payouts, expenses note)

Duplicate the template once; run it every week. No multi-team CRM theatre.

How to run it in 20 minutes a week

  1. Open the weekly dashboard; set one focus sentence.
  2. Move sales cards; close or kill stale leads.
  3. Advance one content piece; park the rest.
  4. Tick money hygiene (fees logged, payout noted).
  5. Write next week’s top three before you close the tab.
